    Grass & foliage

    Oberon paints grass and scattered models (rocks, flowers, trees) straight onto the ground with a brush. What the components do and how they look is in the Grass & Foliage guide. This page is about painting.

    Grass#

    Create → Grass makes an entity with a Grass component, filled over a 10 × 10 m area. To start from bare ground and only paint, set Area Width and Area Depth to 0.

    FieldMeaning
    Area Width / Area DepthThe filled rectangle, metres (0 for none)
    DensityBlades per square metre where fully painted
    Height / Height VaryThe tallest blades, and how much shorter some are
    Blade WidthBlade width at the root
    Root Color / Tip ColorThe blades' colours, root to tip
    Color VaryHow much blades' colours differ
    Draw DistanceHow far from the camera it's drawn
    SeedPicks which random field grows

    Scatter#

    Add a Scatter component (Add Component → Scatter) to any entity with a Mesh Renderer. Its model is then drawn at many spots instead of once. Use a small, low-poly model: every copy costs its triangles.

    FieldMeaning
    Area Width / Area DepthThe filled rectangle, metres
    DensityCopies per square metre where fully painted
    Scale Min / Scale MaxThe range of sizes
    Follow Slope0 = upright, 1 = tilted with the ground
    SeedPicks where the copies go

    The brush#

    Select the entity. Under its Grass or Scatter section in the Inspector:

    1. Click Paint (or Erase). The brush is on, and the move and rotate gizmo steps aside while it is.
    2. Pick a Size: 0.5, 1.5, 3 or 6 m across the disc.
    3. Pick a Strength: 25 %, 50 % or 100 %. Lower strengths paint thinner grass, or erase only part of it.
    4. Drag over the ground in the scene view. A ring shows where the brush will land. It sits on whatever surface is under the cursor (terrain, rocks, a roof) and falls back to the ground plane at height 0. Grass and scattered models painted over a Terrain stand on it, and follow it when it's sculpted more.

    Each drag is one undo step (Ctrl+Z). Click Paint or Erase again to turn the brush off. It's also off during Play.

    Strokes are stored on the component, in the entity's own space, so moving the entity carries what you painted with it. The note under the brush counts the strokes. Painted grass sways in the Environment's Wind.

    Wind#

    The Environment has two rows for the wind that blows through the whole scene:

    FieldMeaning
    Wind0 still to 1 a gale. Grass sways, spring bones stream out, and water throws spray from 0.5 up
    Wind DirectionWhich way it blows, degrees around Y (0 = toward +X)

    A Water component's own Wind row still works on its own; spray follows whichever wind is stronger.

    Try it#

    Open examples/oberon/meadow/level.ron:

    • Select Meadow, click Erase with the 1.5 m brush, and cut a new path. Press Play and walk it (W A S D, Shift to run).
    • Select Poppies, click Paint and add a bed by the trees.
    • Set the Environment's Wind to 1 and watch the field, her ponytail and skirt all go the same way.
