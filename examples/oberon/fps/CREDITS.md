# Credits

Blaster Arena is a remake of Kenney's
[Starter Kit FPS](https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-FPS) (a Godot
template) for AUTOMATA. The gameplay scripts in `scripts/` are new Python
code, but the level layout, tuning values and the player, enemy and weapon
behaviour follow the original.

## Art and sound: CC0

Everything under `models/` (including `models/Textures/colormap.png`),
`sounds/` and `sprites/` is by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and released under
[Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
You may use it in any project, commercial or not, without attribution.
Credit is still appreciated.

The `*.glb.import.ron` files next to some models are AUTOMATA import settings
(rotation and scale), not part of the original assets.
